Featured work

Real materials, emergent phases.

Each project begins with a materials-realistic electronic structure and follows it toward correlation, superconductivity, transport, or device function.

01 Nature · 2024

Polarity-engineered vertical logic.

A CrOCl interface reconfigures the carrier polarity of MoS2, enabling both n-type and p-type transistors to be integrated vertically rather than only scaled across a plane.

Electronic-structure calculations connect the interfacial surface state with the observed device behavior, linking atomically controlled materials physics to three-dimensional complementary logic.

  • Van der Waals interfaces
  • Interface DFT
  • 3D logic

02 Physical Review X · 2026

Interaction-driven topological transitions in TaIrTe4.

Materials-derived tight-binding and Hartree–Fock calculations map metallic, trivial, quantum spin Hall, and higher-order topological phases near the van Hove singularities of monolayer TaIrTe4.

Strain and dielectric screening act as experimentally accessible control parameters, while band-projected exact diagonalization and transport at fractional fillings point toward time-reversal-symmetric fractional topology.

  • Hartree–Fock
  • Exact diagonalization
  • Quantum spin Hall
View publication ↗

03 Nature · 2024

A dual quantum spin Hall insulator.

Electrostatic gating moves monolayer TaIrTe4 between a conventional quantum spin Hall gap and a second gap generated by density-tuned electronic correlations.

The two regimes share helical boundary transport but arise from different microscopic mechanisms, making one material a controllable platform for comparing single-particle and interaction-driven topology.

  • Correlations
  • Helical edges
  • Density tuning
View publication ↗

04 Nature · 2026

Electrically switchable superlattice memory.

Monolayer TaIrTe4 hosts a long-period superlattice that can be written and erased non-volatilely by electrostatic tuning of its low-energy quantum spin Hall electrons.

Coupled but distinct lattice and electronic instabilities enable nanoscale structural memory and switchable correlated states at fractional superlattice fillings.

  • Bistability
  • Superlattice
  • Non-volatile memory

05 Nature Electronics · 2025

A correlated topological semimetal becomes a terahertz detector.

Few-layer TaIrTe4 combines a large room-temperature nonlinear Hall response with terahertz nonlinear electrodynamics, enabling zero-bias detection across 0.1–10 THz.

Electrostatic gating strengthens electronic correlations and sharply improves sensitivity, connecting correlated topological matter with practical broadband and ultrafast sensing.

  • Nonlinear Hall effect
  • THz detection
  • Correlations

06 Nature Communications · 2025

Nonlinear Hall transport maps a correlated topological phase.

Nonlinear Hall measurements reveal a correlated charge-density-wave state in few-layer TaIrTe4 through a dramatic enhancement of nonlinear conductivity and Berry-curvature redistribution.

Raman spectroscopy, second-harmonic generation, and first-principles theory identify a one-dimensional charge order and establish nonlinear transport as a sensitive probe of correlated topological phases.

  • Charge-density wave
  • Berry curvature
  • Nonlinear transport

07 Research preprint · 2025

Intertwining Josephson and vortex topologies.

Direct simulation of a realistic superconducting vortex is computationally prohibitive. A Josephson–vortex correspondence maps the same bound-state topology onto tractable π-junction calculations.

The strategy enables first-principles searches for Majorana platforms without constructing enormous vortex supercells, and was demonstrated for conventional superconductors including 2M-WS2 and Sr3SnO.

  • Majorana modes
  • BdG topology
  • π junctions
View preprint ↗

08 Physical Review B · 2019

Topological massive Dirac fermions in β-tungsten.

First-principles electronic structure revealed 24 symmetry-related massive Dirac fermions generated when spin–orbit coupling gaps an underlying nodal-line structure.

The mechanism connects topology with β-W's large spin Hall response and suggests a possible microscopic origin for this experimentally important transport property.

  • Massive Dirac fermions
  • Spin Hall effect
  • β-W
View publication ↗

09 Science China Materials · 2018

Topological catalysis for hydrogen evolution in TiSi.

TiSi’s Dirac nodal line generates drumhead-like surface states proposed as the active platform for hydrogen evolution, linking band topology directly to surface chemistry.

First-principles calculations predict near-zero hydrogen-adsorption free energies on the (010) and (110) surfaces and trace charge transfer from the topological surface state into adsorbed hydrogen.

  • Topological catalysis
  • Drumhead surface states
  • Hydrogen evolution
