01 Physical Review Letters · 2023

Direct observation of topological phonons in graphene.

Full-zone high-resolution electron energy-loss spectroscopy mapped phonons throughout the two-dimensional Brillouin zone, revealing two nodal rings and the band-crossing signatures of all four predicted Dirac-phonon classes.

The experiment directly confirmed the LA–ZO nodal ring and Dirac phonons predicted in the 2020 PRB study below; it also resolved a second TA–ZO nodal ring.

02 Nature Communications · 2021

A materials-scale atlas of topological phonons.

An automated workflow converted first-principles force constants into band-crossing searches, topological diagnostics, and boundary spectra. Screening more than 10,000 materials produced a catalog of 5,014 topological-phonon candidates.

The database also identified 322 clean systems with isolated topological features, turning individual predictions into a reusable experimental discovery platform.

03 Physical Review B · 2020

Graphene as a theory target for phonon topology.

First-principles calculations identified four classes of Dirac phonons, a Γ-centered LA–ZO nodal ring, and nontrivial edge states in a material whose lattice dynamics can be measured with exceptional momentum resolution.

Those predictions were subsequently tested by full-zone HREELS in the 2023 PRL experiment above, which mapped the ring and associated Dirac crossings and additionally resolved a second TA–ZO nodal ring.

  • First principles
  • Dirac phonons
  • Edge spectra

04 Physical Review B · 2018

New phonon quasiparticles in real crystals.

Calculations for TiS, ZrSe, and HfTe predicted coexisting three-component and two-component Weyl phonons, together with surface arcs that encode their bulk topological charges.

The study established a symmetry-based design language for unconventional lattice excitations and showed that multiple kinds of Weyl phonons can coexist in one materials family.

  • Crystal symmetry
